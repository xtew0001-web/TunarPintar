⌁ How this estimate works

An adaptive view of available cash

The recommendation is recalculated whenever a new payment, changed due date or updated balance affects liquidity. It protects obligations, the reserve and an uncertainty buffer before proposing a sweep.

  • New and floating payments reduce the proposal
  • Reserve and uncertainty buffer stay protected
  • Every sweep remains reviewable before placement
